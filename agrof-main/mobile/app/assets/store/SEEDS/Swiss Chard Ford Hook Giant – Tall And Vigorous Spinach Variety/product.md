# Swiss Chard Ford Hook Giant – Tall And Vigorous Spinach Variety

## Overview
This is a tall, vigorous growing, sturdy variety which produces only leaves and is suitable for home gardens and fresh markets. The leaves are large, broad, dark glossy green and heavily crumped with midribs which are white, thick, succulent and a little broader.

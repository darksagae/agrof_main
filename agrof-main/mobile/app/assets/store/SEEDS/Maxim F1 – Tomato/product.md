# Maxim F1 – Tomato

## Overview
Indeterminate hybrid, tall with vigorous plant habit. Broad, dark green leaves with excellent foliage cover. Ideal for greenhouse or Open field production under support system. Square oblong firm fruits turning deep uniform red colour at ripening stage. Tolerant to Bacterial wilt, Fusarium & Verticillium wilt Stemphylium.

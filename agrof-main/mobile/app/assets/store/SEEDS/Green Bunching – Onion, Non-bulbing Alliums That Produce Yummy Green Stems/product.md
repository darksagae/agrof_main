# Green Bunching – Onion, Non-bulbing Alliums That Produce Yummy Green Stems

## Overview
Green Bunching are perennial non-bulbing alliums that produce yummy green stems and tiny white roots, year after year. They have thick, round, hollow stems that are bright green in color, and unique and lovely greenish white flowers. The leaves have a mild onion flavor and are edible raw or cooked.

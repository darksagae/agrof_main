# Mammoth Red Rock – Red Cabbage Producing Large, Beautiful Deep Red-purple Heads

## Overview
This cabbage produces large, beautiful deep red-purple heads. This easy to grow, low maintenance variety produces a uniformly round head that is 20-25cm in diameter and weighs approximately 2-3.5kgs. It has a small to medium core and grows to a height of around 28cm. It stores well, reportedly loosing neither flavour nor crispness.

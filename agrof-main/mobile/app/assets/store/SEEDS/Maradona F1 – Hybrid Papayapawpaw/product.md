# Maradona F1 – Hybrid Papayapawpaw

## Overview
Maradona F1 is a Hybrid with dwarf and strong plants and high setting ability. It bares uniform fruits (1.5-2kg) in shape and size. Fruit flesh is firm, thick and has a very sweet taste (brix: 12-14). It's dwarf growth habit makes it a superior variety since it does not grow very tall making it easy and cost effective to harvest. Intermediate resistant to PRSV and Anthrachnose. Days to maturity: 210-240. Color: Orange. Flowering habit: Dwarf.

# Merdan F1- African Eggplants

## Overview
Merdan F1 is a fast-growing, vigorous hybrid eggplant variety known for its semi-erect plant habit and medium to late maturity. It produces uniform, very shiny black, firm, oval-shaped fruits ideal for long-distance transport. The fruits typically measure 12-14 cm in length, 9-10 cm in diameter, and weigh between 250-300 grams. Merdan F1 is suitable for both indoor and outdoor cultivation and exhibits high resistance to Tobacco Mosaic Virus.

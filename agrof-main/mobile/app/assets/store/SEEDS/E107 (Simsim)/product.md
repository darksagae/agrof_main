# E107 (Simsim)

## Overview
E107 sesame variety is a mechanizable high yielding, non shattering and drought resistant sesame variety that matures in 3 months. Preferably planting in line or can be broadcasted after clear guidelines from an agronomist. Row to row spacing of 15cm and continuous drilling. Planting: mixing ratio 2.5 kgs of seed to 8kgs of sand and ash for 1 acre. For maximum yields, Use of fertilizers is encouraged. Yields up to 5 bags/acre.

# Green Sprouting Calabrese – Broccoli With Medium Sized Dark Green Heads

## Overview
Green Sprouting Calabrese broccoli has Medium sized dark green heads. Plant continues producing side shoots for several weeks after the main flower head is cut. It is Very nutritious, very tender and has Slow bolting. Produces compact dark green central head, with many lateral or side shoots over a long season.

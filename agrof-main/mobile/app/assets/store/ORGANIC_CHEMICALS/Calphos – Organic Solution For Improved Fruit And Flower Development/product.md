# Calphos – Organic Solution For Improved Fruit Development

## Overview
Calphos is a nutrient solution for plants just entering the flowering cycle. In natural farming, apply Calphos before the flower initiation to reduce flower abortion and support the eventual fruit. Calcium is used to strengthen the plant in preparation for heavy flowers/fruits. Main Ingredients: Natural source of calcium and phosphorous

# Oregano Seedling

## Overview
Oregano is a loose, open plant with gray-green leaves and small purple or white flowers. Oregano is a hardy rampant growing perennial. Oregano is used in sauces, tomato dishes, pizza, Mexican dishes, salads and soups. Oregano is considered a staple herb in many cuisines around the world. It has a strong flavor and brings warmth to dishes, along with a hint of subtle sweetness.

## Overview
Strawberries are low-growing herbaceous plants with a fibrous root system. The flowers, generally white, rarely reddish, are borne in small clusters. Packed with vitamins, fiber, and particularly high levels of antioxidants known as polyphenols. Just one serving -- about eight strawberries -- provides more vitamin C than an orange.

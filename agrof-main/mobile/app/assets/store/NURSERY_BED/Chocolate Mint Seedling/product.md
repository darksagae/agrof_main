# Chocolate Mint Seedling

## Overview
Chocolate mint plants are attractive, fragrant and easy to grow. The plant grows to about 2 feet tall and easily spreads by rhizomes into an attractive ground cover. The rounded, lance-shaped leaves are a darker green than other forms of mint. In cooking, chocolate mint can be used for flavoring desserts and drinks. In landscapes, this fast-growing perennial is often naturalized as a ground cover in moist areas.

# Lemon Grass (Kisubi)

## Overview
Lemon grass is a perennial herb with a light lemon scent and flavor. This grass has a rich flavor of lemon citrus, can be used to brew tea as well as a herb for seasoning. Lemon grass essential oil can be used in aromatherapy, cosmetics, and as natural insecticide. It has a lemony flavour and can be dried and powdered or used fresh. The crop can be harvested continuously for long. Lemongrass can be grown in a pot or any other container and put on the veranda or balcony.

### **Smart Gating System:**

- **3rd attempt onwards**: Authentication required

---

## 🔥 **Enable Firebase (Final Step)**

To make signup/login actually work:

**1. Open Firebase Console:**
```
https://console.firebase.com/project/agrof-ef825/authentication/providers
```

**2. Enable Email/Password:**
- Click "Email/Password"
- Toggle "Enable" to ON
- Click "Save"

**Done!** Takes 30 seconds.

---

## 🧪 **Test Signup**

After enabling Firebase:

1. Tap "Store" tab (3 times to trigger auth)
